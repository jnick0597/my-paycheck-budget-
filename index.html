Paycheck settings

Scheduled paydays repeat automatically every 14 days. The Home screen prompts you to confirm the actual deposit before adding it to your cash. This avoids treating money you have not received as spendable.

Monthly planning

Your category allowances restart every 14-day paycheck period, but unspent cash carries forward automatically. Bills repeat monthly. The app keeps old transactions. Your monthly reserve target is divided across 26 annual paychecks.

Backup & restore

Data stays in this browser on this device. Clearing Safari website data, changing devices, or using private browsing may erase it. Export backups regularly. The app does not connect to your bank.
